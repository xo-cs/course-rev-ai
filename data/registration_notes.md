Starting in 2025, there are significant changes to the curriculum requirements. Please read everything before you proceed to the course registration instructions:

Major Credits
The "Required & Elective" classification will be removed. All credits will now fall under "Major. Major credits will be divided into 4 levels:
• Basic (기초): Courses taken in Year 1.
• Core (핵심): Courses taken in Years 2–4.
• Advanced (심화): Courses taken in Years 2–4.
• Focus (중점): Specialized courses in Years 3–4 leading to certifications or licenses. You must take these courses to graduate.

Class Schedule Adjustment:
•Most 3-hour courses will be divided into two 1.5-hour sessions on different days.
Example: Course Mon, Wed 1:00 pm – 2:30 pm.

[Special Curriculum Programs Credit Updates]

The following Special Curriculum Programs are available this semester:
• Special Curriculum Program 1 [Nano-PAMS]: Up to 6 credits (2 courses ONLY)
• Special Curriculum Program 2 [Korean Lectures & K-POP]: Up to 6 credits (2 courses ONLY)
• Special Curriculum Program 3 [Sol-Connection]: 3 credits (Only those who submitted application form)

For example, you can take the courses like this:
Special Curriculum Program 1: 6 credits + Special Curriculum Program 2: 3 credits + GE: 3 credits + Track: 6 credit = 18 credits 

Important Notes:
• Each Special Curriculum Programs limit is 6 credits, but there is no limit for the overall Special Curriculum Program credit.
• All these courses/credits are transferable:
     • For minor/track, only a maximum of 6 credits can be transferred.
• Please be aware:
     • If a credit you plan to replace is canceled for this semester, it cannot be replaced and will remain as major credit.
• FOR EXAMPLE:
     • If a GE(4) credit is canceled this semester, it cannot be replaced as GE(4) and will stay as major credit.
